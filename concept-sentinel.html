What this concept explores

📡

Watch everything, show little

The board watches every system but surfaces only changes, so a calm screen genuinely means a calm system.

🚨

Alert fatigue is a design bug

Warnings that resolve themselves get logged, not paged. A person is interrupted only when a person is needed.

🧮

A feed with memory

The event stream is also the audit trail: what happened, when, and what cleared it, all in one place.

🌑

Built for 3 a.m.

Status is readable at a glance by someone who just woke up: three lights, one feed, no interpretation required.
