What this concept explores

🏡

Goals as first-class objects

Not one big balance, but named goals with their own pace, target date, and finish line.

📅

Time made visible

Every goal answers "when do I get there at this pace" without anyone doing the math.

🌱

Motion as encouragement

Rings fill and bars grow in the direction of the habit. The interface celebrates the deposit, not the dashboard.

🔒

Calm by default

No streaks to lose, no guilt mechanics. Numbers stay warm, honest, and easy to read.
