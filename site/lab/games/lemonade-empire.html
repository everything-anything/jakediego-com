How to Play 📖

🌤️ Check the forecast. Hot days = LOTS of thirsty customers. Rainy days = almost none. (The forecast is sometimes wrong!)

🛒 Buy supplies. One Lemonade Kit makes 5 cups and costs $5, so each cup costs you $1 to make. Sell it for more than $1 and you earn profit!

💲 Set your price. Cheap prices bring more customers. High prices earn more per cup… but scare people away. That's supply and demand!

🏦 Use your Rainy Day Jar. Money in the jar is SAFE, sneaky robbers and bad days can't touch it. Smart bosses always save some!

🌙 Watch out at night. Surprises happen. Your choices matter!
