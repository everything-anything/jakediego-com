Case study

Customer-success command center for a services company

Problem: the customer-success team was tracking at-risk customers, renewals, onboarding, and email follow-ups across scattered spreadsheets and inboxes. There was no single place to see who needed attention, and no way to measure whether the team's work was actually preventing cancellations.

What was built: a command-center dashboard covering customer health tracking, an at-risk flagging workflow, automated email drafting with a calibrated writing voice, and an internal value/ROI engine that credits the customer-success system with the revenue it retains.

Verified outcomes: 33 at-risk customers were flagged and tracked to an outcome (21 retained, 4 churned, 8 still open) with $30,590 in retained revenue credited to the workflow, and a companion automation loop cut the run time of the team's email-drafting pipeline by 45.9% in measured testing.

Case study

Automated market intelligence pipeline

Problem: spotting where a market is growing, or where a competitive gap is opening up, was a slow, manual, once-a-quarter research exercise that got inconsistent results and went stale fast.

What was built: a scheduled, four-stage pipeline (collection, signal scoring, synthesis, ranked output) that pulls public market data automatically and hands back a ranked opportunity report with no manual pull required.
